These are not the same kind of edge LS-LR-NVAR estimates a contemporaneous association network (probit latents plus a |corr| ≥ 0.35 floor). Chen estimates time-varying Granger links after L1 shrinkage. A denser LS-LR-NVAR graph is expected; the interesting comparison is how topology and time variation differ once that is granted.

Average topology

LS-LR-NVARassociation
Almost complete, nearly undirected, and egalitarian: ~684 of 870 directed edges, reciprocity 99.7%, clustering 86.5%, Gini 0.12, one connected component. Highest out-degree names stay HD, CSCO, HON, SHW.

Density moves only 78.3–79.0% over nine years. The correlation floor and a tightly clipped RW step on the dynamic latents keep the graph nearly static.

Factor-adjusted LASSOprimary Chen spec
After removing three return factors, residual Granger structure is thin: mean 9 edges, density 1.1%, reciprocity 1.3%. The graph is often empty or a few spokes. Mean largest-component share is 24%.

Two mild density bumps: 2019–2020 (peak 3.1%) and late 2025–early 2026 (end 2.2%, hubs MMM and AMZN).

Direct LASSOrobustness
Raw-return Granger graphs are denser and more cyclic: mean density 9.8%, clustering 28.6%, largest component 76%. Reciprocity is 36% around 2020 and zero after 2022.

Common factors create many contemporaneous-looking lags. End-sample hubs are GS (17), AXP (13), JPM (11).

Reciprocity and clustering

Reciprocity

Share of directed edges that have a reverse edge. LS-LR-NVAR is essentially undirected. Direct LASSO is mutual only in the 2019–2021 dense spell.

Undirected clustering

Global transitivity on the symmetrized graph. Direct LASSO triangles peak with COVID-era density; factor-adjusted triangles are rare.

Concentration and connectivity

Out-degree Gini

LS-LR-NVAR degrees are nearly equal (Gini ~0.12). LASSO graphs are hub-and-spoke (Gini 0.6–0.9). Factor-adjusted Gini is 0 when the graph is empty.

Largest weakly connected component

LS-LR-NVAR is always one component. Direct LASSO is fully connected in 2019–mid-2021, then fragments. Factor-adjusted never exceeds 63% of names.

Protocol

LS-LR-NVAR

month-end of daily posterior-mean A_t (threshold 0.5). Blocked Gibbs, 24 sweeps, 12 burn-in, thin 2 (6 kept draws). Topology at probability 0.5. Same correlation floor and tau clip as the 6-month forecast program.

Chen TV-LASSO

36 local-linear LASSO Granger graphs, tau in [0.18, 1]. Equation-wise LassoCV once at tau = 1, then that lambda reused on the grid with local-linear Epanechnikov weights (h = 0.20, p = 2). Own lags are not counted as Granger edges.
