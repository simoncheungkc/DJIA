Protocol and caveats

Fair comparison

Same split as before: 2,387 training return days, last train price 2026-02-05, 126 open-loop steps. Chen coefficients are estimated once at the origin. LS-LR-NVAR uses the posterior mean of the recursive mean path (blocked Gibbs, 24 sweeps, 6 kept draws after burn-in).

What this is not

Gibbs used a correlation-threshold floor on the probit network and a local-mean clip so multi-step return paths stay in a realistic daily range. Chen CLIME partial-correlation networks are unused because the target is the mean price path. Rolling one-step notebook MAPE is a different protocol.
