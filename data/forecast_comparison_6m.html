Holdout 2026-02-06 to 2026-08-07 (126 trading days, 30 DJIA names). Open-loop prices trained through 2026-02-05. LS-LR-NVAR uses specification Gibbs Steps 0–10 and Step 11 predictive simulation on log-returns. TVP-LR-NVAR-SV iterates log-prices; Chen and BCVAR iterate daily log-returns then exponentiate.

Models LS-LR-NVAR and TVP-LR-NVAR-SV use a Minnesota predictive mean: 5% annual common premium, one-third of historical name-mean deviations, and the frozen origin network VAR. That replaces unconstrained random-walk intercepts (spec) and fading 21-day momentum (TVP v2), which over- or under-shot six-month prices. Chen factor-adjusted is the paper's recommended equity spec (PCA k=3 then sparse TV-VAR). Chen direct is sparse TV-VAR on raw returns. BCVAR / BCVAR-TVP are Koop, Korobilis and Pettenuzzo (JoE 2019).
